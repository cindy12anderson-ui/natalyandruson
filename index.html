About
Nataly Andruson Architecture & Interior Design is an architecture and interior design studio specialising in high-end private residences, multi-family residential developments, and commercial interiors.

The studio is led by Nataly Andruson, an Architectural Practical Engineer and Interior Designer with over 15 years of professional experience. Before founding the studio, Nataly worked at leading architectural practices in Israel, gaining extensive expertise across luxury private residences, multi-family residential developments, and commercial interiors. This multidisciplinary experience continues to shape a thoughtful, detail-oriented approach to every project.

Every project is personally led by Nataly, ensuring continuity of vision, precision, and close collaboration throughout the design process.

Philosophy
Architecture and interior design are approached as a single, continuous design process, where every decision contributes to a cohesive whole.

Thoughtful planning forms the foundation of every project. Guided by clarity, proportion, natural materials, and meticulous attention to detail, the studio creates architecture and interiors that are functional, timeless, and carefully considered.

Rather than following trends, the studio is committed to creating spaces designed to endure, balancing functionality, proportion, and lasting quality.

Approach

  1. 01

    Understanding

    The site, its light, and the people who will live within it.

  2. 02

    Design

    Proportion, material, and detail, resolved as one whole.

  3. 03

    Realisation

    Carried through to completion, with the same care at every scale.

Every successful project begins with careful planning and the right collaboration.
